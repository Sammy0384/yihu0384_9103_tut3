# Quiz 8

## Part 1 — Imaging Technique Inspiration

I am inspired by teamLab and their work Universe of Water Particles on a Rock where People Gather. They created immersive electronic art works in a dreamlike digital space using floating particles, glowing light, and layered transparency. I particularly enjoyed the highly atmospheric, dynamic, soft light, and abstract visual depth. I hope to combine drifting particles, dynamic blurring, and reactive light in my project to create a visually rich and interactive experience with emotional depth. I think this technology is fascinating because it combines abstraction, dynamics, and audience interaction in a way which is highly suitable for creative programming and experimental media design.

## Part 2 — Coding Technique Exploration

To recreate these atmospheric visual effects, I searched the flow field particles, interactive fluid simulation, and audio reactive visuals in p5.js. Flow fields and Perlin noise can generate smooth organic motion similar to drifting light and water currents, while particle system create layered and immersive visual depth. I want to combine transparency, blurring and interaction to produce immersive generative visual effects. It will be better to add mouse movement or sound input to affect the behavior of the particles, creating a more interactive and emotional digital experience.
